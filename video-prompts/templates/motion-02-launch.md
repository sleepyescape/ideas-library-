# Запуски продуктов и промо

**Для:** Claude Motion · Claude Code + Remotion / HyperFrames

Ролики для приложений, сервисов и фич. Подставь свой продукт и экраны, лучше прикрепить скриншоты.

Замени всё в `[КВАДРАТНЫХ СКОБКАХ]` на своё. Остальное копируй как есть.

## Одна фигура — весь интерфейс

Одна форма морфит в кнопку, лоадер, плеер, график. Ведёт курсор. `16:9` · ~20 с

```text
A 20-second black-and-white product film for [PRODUCT]. A single rounded rectangle morphs continuously through UI states driven by a cursor: button → loading spinner → toggle → slider → music player → bar chart → notification. Each state lasts ~2.5 s and the cursor clicks to trigger the next. Spring physics on every morph. Final state: the shape becomes the [PRODUCT] logo with the tagline "[TAGLINE]".
```

## Запуск одним дублем

Без склеек: экраны, фото и текст перетекают друг в друга. `16:9` · ~30 с

```text
Create a 30-second launch film for [PRODUCT] in one continuous camera move, no cuts. Start on a close-up of [KEY OBJECT/SCREEN], pull back to reveal the app UI, scroll through [FEATURE 1], [FEATURE 2], [FEATURE 3] as the camera glides; text callouts appear attached to the UI. Warm off-white canvas, soft shadows, one accent colour [ACCENT]. End on the logo and "[CTA]".
```

## Ввёл запрос — получил результат

Пользователь печатает, интерфейс сам собирает ответ. `1:1` · ~12 с

```text
A 12-second square product sting for [PRODUCT]. A search/prompt box types "[USER QUERY]" character by character. On Enter, the result page builds itself: header, then [RESULT CARD 1], [RESULT CARD 2], [RESULT CARD 3] stack in with a 60 ms stagger. Two feature badges "[FEATURE A]" and "[FEATURE B]" slide in. Last frame: logo + URL. Light UI, crisp shadows.
```

## Тизер новой фичи

Интрига: размытый экран, обратный отсчёт, раскрытие. `9:16` · ~15 с

```text
A 15-second vertical teaser for a new [PRODUCT] feature. 0–5 s: blurred, cropped glimpses of the new screen with the text "Something new is coming". 5–10 s: countdown 3-2-1 with each number punching in. 10–15 s: the blur clears revealing [FEATURE NAME] in action, with the date "[LAUNCH DATE]" below. Dark theme, accent glow [ACCENT].
```

## Запуск в стиле твита

Ролик собран из карточек-постов и лайков. `16:9` · ~20 с

```text
Create a 20-second SaaS launch video that looks like a social feed. Post cards appear one after another: a founder post "We just launched [PRODUCT]", reply bubbles with reactions, a like counter rolling up to [NUMBER], a quote card with a customer saying "[QUOTE]". Cards slide and stack with springy motion. End: big "[PRODUCT] — [TAGLINE]" and a link.
```

## Прогулка по приложению

Телефон в кадре, экраны листаются, подписи-выноски. `9:16` · ~25 с

```text
A 25-second vertical walkthrough of the [APP NAME] mobile app. A phone mockup sits centre; screens change with smooth swipes: onboarding → [SCREEN 1] → [SCREEN 2] → [SCREEN 3]. A finger-tap ripple shows each interaction. Short captions above the phone (max 5 words each) explain the benefit, not the feature. Final screen: App Store / Google Play badges and "[CTA]". Use the attached screenshots if provided.
```

## Реферальная программа

Пригласи друга — получи бонус: две аватарки и монетки. `1:1` · ~12 с

```text
A 12-second square promo for a referral programme. Two friendly avatars sit left and right; a link icon flies from one to the other, then coins / gift boxes burst for both with the numbers "[REWARD YOU]" and "[REWARD FRIEND]". Big headline "Invite a friend, get [REWARD]". Bright, rounded, playful, brand colour [COLOUR].
```

## Эмоциональное промо

Не про функции, а про чувство: история одного пользователя. `9:16` · ~30 с

```text
A 30-second emotional promo for [PRODUCT], told without showing the UI until the end. Minimal illustrated scenes of one person's day: [PROBLEM MOMENT] → frustration (muted colours) → discovering a small helper → [GOOD MOMENT] (colours warm up). One line of text per scene, max 6 words. Only in the last 5 seconds the app screen appears with the logo and "[TAGLINE]". Soft piano-friendly pacing.
```

## Тарифы

Три тарифа появляются слева направо, выделяется лучший. `16:9` · ~20 с

```text
A 20-second animation of [PRODUCT] pricing. Three plan cards appear left to right: [PLAN 1, PRICE], [PLAN 2, PRICE], [PLAN 3, PRICE], each with three checkmark features that tick on. Then the middle card lifts, gets a glowing border and a "Most popular" badge; a toggle flips from monthly to annual and prices count down to show the [DISCOUNT] saving. End: "Start free" button pulse.
```

## До и после

Разделённый экран: хаос без продукта vs порядок с ним. `9:16` · ~15 с

```text
A 15-second vertical before/after. A vertical divider splits the screen. Left "Before": a messy stack of [PAIN ITEMS] (tabs, notes, spreadsheets) jittering chaotically in grey. Right "After": the same items neatly organised inside [PRODUCT] in colour. The divider sweeps left until the "After" side fills the frame. Caption: "[BENEFIT IN 5 WORDS]". Logo at the end.
```

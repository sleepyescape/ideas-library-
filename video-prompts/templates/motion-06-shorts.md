# Форматы для Reels / TikTok / Shorts

**Для:** Claude Motion · Claude Code + Remotion / HyperFrames

Вертикаль 9:16, крючок в первые 2 секунды, крупный текст. Работают и как код-анимация, и как основа для генераторов.

Замени всё в `[КВАДРАТНЫХ СКОБКАХ]` на своё. Остальное копируй как есть.

## Топ-5 с обратным отсчётом

5 → 1, на первом месте сюрприз. `9:16` · ~30 с

```text
A 30-second vertical "Top 5 [TOPIC]" countdown. Hook (0–2 s): "Number 1 will surprise you". Then #5 to #1, ~5 s each: a big rank number slams in, the item name, one punchy fact, a simple icon or illustration. #1 gets a bigger reveal with a flash. Bold captions centred, safe from the top 15 % and bottom 20 % of the screen (UI zones).
```

## А вы знали?

Один удивительный факт, разобранный за 15 секунд. `9:16` · ~15 с

```text
A 15-second vertical "Did you know?" clip about [FACT]. 0–2 s hook: the surprising statement in huge type. 2–11 s: three quick visual beats explaining why it's true. 11–15 s: "Follow for more [TOPIC]" with a small arrow. Big, high-contrast captions, one idea per screen.
```

## POV-история текстом

«POV: ты …» и сценки из коротких реплик. `9:16` · ~20 с

```text
A 20-second vertical POV story made of on-screen text and simple illustrations. Opening caption "POV: you [SITUATION]". Then 5 beats, each a short line of dialogue or thought ([BEAT 1..5]) in chat-bubble or meme-caption style, with a quick zoom on the punchline. Ends on a relatable twist line "[TWIST]".
```

## Переписка в мессенджере

История через чат: сообщения появляются с набором. `9:16` · ~25 с

```text
A 25-second vertical animated phone chat between [PERSON A] and [PERSON B]. Messages appear one by one with a typing indicator first; timing feels natural. Script: [PASTE DIALOGUE]. The last message is the punchline and gets a little shake + emoji reaction. Use a generic messenger look (not a real brand's UI).
```

## Это или то

Два варианта, голосование, счётчик процентов. `9:16` · ~20 с

```text
A 20-second vertical "This or That" game with 4 rounds about [TOPIC]. Each round: the screen splits top/bottom with [OPTION A] and [OPTION B], a 3-second timer ring, then fake percentage bars fill to show "what most people chose". Bright contrasting colours for the two halves, big emoji.
```

## Лайфхак за 3 шага

Проблема → 3 действия → результат. `9:16` · ~20 с

```text
A 20-second vertical life-hack clip: "How to [GOAL] in 3 steps". Hook shows the problem [PROBLEM]. Steps 1–3 each have a big number, an icon and a 6-word instruction. Final screen shows the result and "Save this for later". Clean, friendly, tons of whitespace.
```

## Угадай за 3 секунды

Размытая картинка/силуэт проясняется по таймеру. `9:16` · ~25 с

```text
A 25-second vertical guessing quiz: "Guess the [CATEGORY] in 3 seconds". 5 rounds: a heavily blurred or silhouetted shape of [ITEM] appears, a 3-2-1 timer, then it un-blurs with the answer label. Score prompt "How many did you get?" at the end. Use simple illustrated shapes, not real photos or logos.
```

## Цитата дня

Красивая цитата с медленным движением и фоном. `9:16` · ~10 с

```text
A 10-second vertical quote card: "[QUOTE]" — [AUTHOR]. Words fade in one by one in an elegant serif, the background is a slow-moving abstract gradient in [MOOD COLOURS]. The author name appears last in small caps. Calm pacing, perfect for a loop.
```

## История в эмодзи

Сюжет фильма/события рассказан эмодзи. `9:16` · ~20 с

```text
A 20-second vertical clip telling the story of [MOVIE/EVENT/DAY] using only big emoji sequences, 2–3 emoji per beat, 6 beats, each with a short caption. Emoji bounce, rotate and interact (chase, collide, hug). Final card: "Guess the [MOVIE]?" or the answer. Pastel background that changes per beat.
```

## Один день из жизни

Таймлайн дня: часы и сценки. `9:16` · ~30 с

```text
A 30-second vertical "A day in the life of a [ROLE]". A clock in the corner jumps through the day: [07:00 — ACTIVITY], [09:00 — ACTIVITY], [13:00 — ACTIVITY], [18:00 — ACTIVITY], [23:00 — ACTIVITY]. Each moment is a simple flat scene with a one-line caption. Colour temperature shifts from morning cool to evening warm.
```

# Логотипы, интро, заставки

**Для:** Claude Motion · Claude Code + Remotion / HyperFrames

Короткие 3–10-секундные кусочки: начало и конец ролика, смена сцен, брендинг канала.

Замени всё в `[КВАДРАТНЫХ СКОБКАХ]` на своё. Остальное копируй как есть.

## Логотип собирается медленно

Детали логотипа собираются из линий и точек. `16:9` · ~8 с

```text
An 8-second logo reveal for [BRAND]. Start with scattered points and thin lines on a dark background; they slowly drift, connect and assemble into the logo [DESCRIBE LOGO OR ATTACH SVG]. When complete, a soft light sweep passes across it and the name [BRAND] fades in below with letter spacing tightening. Calm, premium pacing.
```

## Интро канала

3 секунды: имя канала, фирменный цвет, звуковой акцент. `16:9` · ~4 с

```text
A 4-second YouTube channel intro for [CHANNEL NAME]. A shape in [BRAND COLOUR] bursts from the centre, wipes the frame, and the channel name snaps in with a bounce and a short shake on impact (the moment a sound hit would land at 1.2 s). Subtle particles linger. Energetic, readable on mobile.
```

## Конечная заставка

Подпишись + две карточки следующих видео. `16:9` · ~10 с

```text
A 10-second YouTube end screen for [CHANNEL]. Leave two empty 16:9 slots on the right for video cards and a circle slot for the subscribe button (they will be added by YouTube). Left side: "Thanks for watching" and [CHANNEL HANDLE] animate in; background has a slow looping gradient and floating shapes in brand colours [COLOURS].
```

## Плашка с именем (lower third)

Имя и должность спикера внизу кадра. `16:9` · ~6 с

```text
Design an animated lower third: a bar slides in from the left, then "[NAME]" and "[TITLE]" appear with a mask reveal; hold 4 s; exit to the left. Transparent background so it can overlay video. Brand colour [COLOUR], sans-serif type, works on light and dark footage.
```

## Набор переходов

5 переходов между сценами в фирменном стиле. `16:9` · ~10 с

```text
Create a pack of five 2-second brand transitions in [BRAND COLOURS]: 1) diagonal stripe wipe, 2) circle iris in/out, 3) liquid blob morph, 4) grid of squares flipping, 5) shape zoom-through. Each one fully covers the frame at its midpoint (so a cut can hide underneath). Play them in sequence with a label for each.
```

## Глитч-логотип

Цифровые помехи, RGB-сдвиг, затем чистый логотип. `1:1` · ~5 с

```text
A 5-second glitch logo sting for [BRAND]. The logo appears corrupted: RGB channel split, horizontal slice offsets, scan lines and flickering blocks for 2 seconds, then snaps to a clean, stable logo with a short chromatic flash. Black background, logo in white with [ACCENT] highlights.
```

## Бумажная аппликация

Логотип из слоёв бумаги с тенями. `1:1` · ~6 с

```text
A 6-second paper cut-out style logo reveal: layers of coloured paper slide in from different sides, each casting a soft drop shadow, and assemble into [LOGO DESCRIPTION]. Slight paper texture and a tiny wobble as each layer settles. Warm palette [COLOURS].
```

## Юбилей бренда

Цифра юбилея, архив лет, праздничный финал. `16:9` · ~15 с

```text
A 15-second anniversary film for [BRAND]'s [N]th year. A year counter flips from [FOUNDING YEAR] to [CURRENT YEAR] while small milestone captions pass by ([MILESTONE 1], [MILESTONE 2], [MILESTONE 3]). The final number [N] builds out of the brand's shapes, then "[N] years of [WHAT]" and a thank-you line. Gold accents on [BRAND COLOUR].
```

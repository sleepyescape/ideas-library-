# Реклама и UGC

**Для:** Veo · Sora · Kling · Runway · Seedance · Hailuo · Wan

Короткие продающие ролики. Не изображай реальных людей и чужие бренды; показывай свой продукт и вымышленных героев.

Замени всё в `[КВАДРАТНЫХ СКОБКАХ]` на своё. Остальное копируй как есть.

## UGC-отзыв

«Обычный человек» рассказывает о продукте на телефон. `9:16` · ~10 с

```text
UGC style, vertical phone footage, front camera. A friendly [AGE] [PERSON DESCRIPTION, fictional] sits in their [CAR / BEDROOM / KITCHEN] holding [PRODUCT] and says naturally: "Okay, I didn't expect this, but [PRODUCT] actually [BENEFIT]." Casual daylight, slight handheld shake, authentic, not over-produced. Burned-in captions.
```

## Проблема → решение

Боль за 2 секунды, продукт решает, результат. `9:16` · ~15 с

```text
SHOT 1 (3 s): close-up of frustration: [PROBLEM, e.g. tangled cables / spilled coffee]. SHOT 2 (6 s): hands use [PRODUCT], the problem disappears smoothly. SHOT 3 (6 s): satisfied result shot, product hero on a clean surface, text "[BENEFIT]". Bright, crisp commercial lighting, upbeat.
```

## Распаковка

Руки открывают коробку, детали крупно. `9:16` · ~10 с

```text
Top-down overhead shot of hands unboxing [PRODUCT] on a [SURFACE]. The lid lifts slowly, tissue paper unfolds, the product is revealed and lifted toward the camera; then a close-up of [KEY DETAIL]. Soft daylight, satisfying ASMR pacing. Sound: paper rustle, a gentle click.
```

## Еда как в рекламе

Сырный тянущийся кадр, пар, капли. `16:9` · ~6 с

```text
Food commercial macro shot. [DISH] on a rustic plate; a fork lifts a piece and [HERO MOMENT: cheese stretches / sauce drips / steam rises] in slow motion. Warm backlight, glistening highlights, dark moody background, shallow depth of field. Sound: sizzle and a soft crunch.
```

## Косметика: всплеск

Флакон, вода, лепестки — премиум-эстетика. `9:16` · ~6 с

```text
Luxury beauty commercial. A [PRODUCT: serum bottle / lipstick] falls in slow motion into clear water, creating a crown splash; [INGREDIENT: rose petals / citrus slices] swirl around it. Pastel [COLOUR] background, soft studio light, pristine reflections. Final 1 s: product upright, perfectly still.
```

## Приложение в реальной жизни

Человек пользуется телефоном, интерфейс «вылетает» в мир. `9:16` · ~8 с

```text
A person in [PLACE] looks at their phone; floating holographic UI elements from [APP TYPE] emerge from the screen into the real room: [UI ELEMENTS: cards / maps / reminders]. They swipe one in the air with their finger. Natural light, realistic, subtle VFX glow. Upbeat modern feel.
```

## Ролик для местного бизнеса

Кофейня/барбершоп/студия: атмосфера и люди. `9:16` · ~15 с

```text
Three-shot promo for a local [BUSINESS: coffee shop / barber / flower studio] called [NAME]. SHOT 1: exterior at morning light, door opens. SHOT 2: close-up of craft [ACTION: latte art / clipper fade / tying a bouquet]. SHOT 3: happy customer receives the result, smiles. Warm, authentic, handheld-cinematic. Text at the end: "[NAME] · [ADDRESS/HANDLE]".
```

## Продукт-великан

Огромный продукт посреди города — сюрреалистичная реклама. `9:16` · ~8 с

```text
Surreal commercial: a giant [PRODUCT] the size of a building stands in the middle of [CITY SQUARE]; people walk around it and take photos; birds fly past; the camera cranes up to reveal its full size. Photorealistic, daylight, slight haze for scale. CGI-ad style (out-of-home campaign look).
```

# Персонажи, мультики, истории

**Для:** Veo · Sora · Kling · Runway · Seedance · Hailuo · Wan

Секрет сериальности — одинаковое описание героя в каждом промпте. Опиши героя один раз и копируй строку дословно (см. плагин cartoon-reel).

Замени всё в `[КВАДРАТНЫХ СКОБКАХ]` на своё. Остальное копируй как есть.

## Карточка героя

Опиши героя один раз — вставляй во все промпты. 

```text
CHARACTER: [NAME], a [AGE / SPECIES] with [BODY SHAPE], [HAIR / FUR], wearing [OUTFIT], always has [SIGNATURE ITEM]. Personality: [3 TRAITS]. Style: [3D clay / stylised 3D cartoon / flat 2D / anime / pixel art]. Colour palette: [3 COLOURS]. — Paste this exact line at the start of every image and video prompt for the series.
```

## Пластилиновый маскот объясняет

Глиняный герой рассказывает о сложном на столе. `9:16` · ~8 с

```text
Stop-motion claymation style. [CHARACTER LINE]. The character stands on a wooden desk among oversized everyday objects and points at [PROP] while explaining [TOPIC] with lively hand gestures. Visible fingerprints in the clay, slightly choppy 12 fps motion, warm practical lighting. Medium shot. Dialogue: "[LINE]".
```

## 3D-мультик: эмоция

Крупный план мультяшного героя с яркой эмоцией. `16:9` · ~6 с

```text
3D animated feature style (stylised, not a specific studio). Close-up of [CHARACTER LINE] reacting to [EVENT]: eyes widen, ears/eyebrows rise, a slow smile spreads. Soft subsurface skin, big expressive eyes, warm bounce light, shallow depth of field. Sound: a small surprised gasp and a soft musical sting.
```

## Аниме-экшен

Динамичный аниме-кадр со скоростными линиями. `16:9` · ~6 с

```text
Japanese anime style, hand-drawn look. [CHARACTER LINE] leaps from [PLACE] toward the camera, cape and hair whipping, speed lines and impact frames, a burst of [ELEMENT: wind / sparks / petals]. Dramatic low angle, sky full of clouds. Sound: whoosh, impact and a short battle shout.
```

## Говорящий предмет

Оживший предмет (кружка, кот, растение) говорит в камеру. `9:16` · ~8 с

```text
A [OBJECT: coffee mug / houseplant / old phone] with a cute cartoon face comes to life on a kitchen counter and talks directly to camera: "[LINE]". Mouth moves in sync, eyes blink, it hops slightly on key words. Realistic kitchen background, soft daylight, playful tone. Voice: [VOICE: grumpy old man / cheerful kid / calm narrator].
```

## Сказочная сцена

Иллюстрация-сказка, как из детской книги. `16:9` · ~8 с

```text
Storybook watercolour illustration style, gently animated. [CHARACTER LINE] walks through [MAGICAL PLACE: glowing mushroom forest / floating islands / snowy village] while [SMALL MAGIC: fireflies follow / leaves swirl / lanterns light up]. Slow parallax camera pan, soft paper texture, warm cosy palette. Narrator: "[STORY LINE]".
```

## Мини-драма из 3 кадров

Завязка, поворот, развязка — 3 промпта подряд. `9:16` · ~24 с

```text
SHOT 1 (8 s): [CHARACTER LINE] in [PLACE] does [NORMAL ACTION]; something small goes wrong: [INCITING MOMENT]. Medium shot.
SHOT 2 (8 s): close-up, they react; the tension rises: [COMPLICATION]. Handheld, faster.
SHOT 3 (8 s): wide shot, the unexpected twist [TWIST], everyone freezes, then laughs/sighs. Same lighting and wardrobe in all three shots.
```

## Герой и напарник: диалог

Двое в кадре спорят/шутят. `9:16` · ~10 с

```text
Two-shot. [CHARACTER A LINE] and [CHARACTER B LINE] sit side by side on [PLACE]. A: "[LINE A]". B (deadpan): "[LINE B]". A reacts with a big comedic double-take. Stylised [STYLE], warm lighting, camera slowly pushes in on the punchline. Clear lip-sync, natural pauses.
```

## Питомец со сверхспособностью

Кот/собака внезапно делает невозможное. `9:16` · ~8 с

```text
Realistic home video look, phone camera. A [PET: ginger cat / corgi] sits on the sofa, then casually [SUPERPOWER: levitates the remote / types on a laptop / opens the fridge with telekinesis] while the owner's hand freezes in frame. Natural living room light, slight handheld shake, comedic timing. Sound: TV in background, a stunned "…what?".
```

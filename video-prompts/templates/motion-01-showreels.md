# Шоурилы и кинетическая типографика

**Для:** Claude Motion · Claude Code + Remotion / HyperFrames

Короткие яркие ролики из текста, фигур и ритма. Хороши для портфолио, интро канала и проверки стиля.

Замени всё в `[КВАДРАТНЫХ СКОБКАХ]` на своё. Остальное копируй как есть.

## Шоурил под бит

15 секунд кинетики: слова, фигуры и склейки строго на долю. `16:9` · ~15 с

```text
Create a 15-second motion design showreel at 120 BPM. Every cut, scale pop and colour flip lands on a beat (one beat = 0.5 s). Act 1 (0–5 s): the words [WORD 1], [WORD 2], [WORD 3] slam in one per beat in heavy condensed type. Act 2 (5–11 s): circles, bars and triangles morph into each other with match cuts. Act 3 (11–15 s): everything collapses into [NAME / LOGO TEXT] on a solid [ACCENT COLOUR] background. Palette: black, off-white, [ACCENT COLOUR]. No easing longer than 0.4 s, no idle frames.
```

## Швейцарский стиль

Строгая сетка, красный акцент, гротеск. Для дизайнеров. `16:9` · ~20 с

```text
Make a 20-second Swiss International Style motion piece. A visible 12-column grid draws itself first. Then [HEADLINE] is set in a neo-grotesk, flush left, and every element snaps to the grid: numbers, rules, a single red square. Motion is only translate and mask reveals, linear or ease-out, no bounce. End frame: [NAME], [ROLE], [YEAR] in a tidy information block. Background off-white #F2F0EB, text black, accent #E2231A.
```

## Громкий хром

Хромовые буквы, чёрный фон, жёсткие склейки. Агрессивно и дорого. `9:16` · ~12 с

```text
A 12-second vertical showreel of loud kinetic typography. Huge chrome-gradient letters on pure black spell [WORD 1] / [WORD 2] / [WORD 3], each word filling the frame. Hard cuts every 0.5 s, occasional 2-frame white flash, letters stretch and skew on entry. Add a subtle film grain and a reflective sheen that sweeps across the letters. Finish on [CALL TO ACTION] in small white caps.
```

## 12 принципов анимации

Сжатие, растяжение, упреждение, оверлап на простых фигурах. `1:1` · ~20 с

```text
Create a 20-second reel that demonstrates animation principles on simple shapes: squash and stretch (a bouncing ball), anticipation (a square that winds up before jumping), follow-through (a pendulum with a trailing tail), ease in / ease out (two dots racing, one linear, one eased), overshoot (a card scaling to 110 % then settling). Each principle gets 4 seconds and a small label in the corner. Flat pastel palette, white background.
```

## Одно слово — десять стилей

Слово меняет шрифт и стиль каждые полсекунды. `9:16` · ~10 с

```text
A 10-second vertical clip where the single word [WORD] stays centred while its style changes every 0.5 s: serif, pixel, neon, handwritten, 3D extrude, glitch, outlined, stencil, bubble, chrome. Background colour changes with each style. Last 2 seconds the word settles in [FINAL STYLE] with a soft glow and the caption [CAPTION] fades in below.
```

## Шрифт, форма, пространство

Три акта: от плоского текста к 3D-пространству. `16:9` · ~15 с

```text
A 15-second showreel in three acts. TYPE (0–5 s): the letters of [WORD] arrange and rearrange in flat 2D. FORM (5–10 s): the letters break into geometric primitives that rotate in pseudo-3D. SPACE (10–15 s): the camera pulls back through a field of those shapes floating in depth with perspective and fog, revealing [NAME] at the centre. Monochrome with one accent colour [ACCENT].
```

## Резюме-ролик

Видео-визитка: имя, роль, навыки, контакты. `16:9` · ~20 с

```text
Create a 20-second motion self-introduction. 0–4 s: "Hi, I'm [NAME]" types itself on with a blinking cursor. 4–9 s: role "[ROLE]" and three skill chips [SKILL 1], [SKILL 2], [SKILL 3] pop in with overshoot. 9–15 s: three project cards [PROJECT 1/2/3] slide in from the right with tiny looping previews (abstract shapes are fine). 15–20 s: a contact card with [EMAIL / LINK] and a friendly wave emoji. Clean, light theme, rounded corners.
```

## Нарочито ужасный шоурил

Смешная пародия: WordArt, звёздные вайпы, Comic Sans. `16:9` · ~15 с

```text
Make a deliberately terrible 15-second showreel for comedy. Use every cliché: WordArt rainbow text saying [TEXT], star wipes, spinning 3D cube transitions, lens flares, Comic Sans subtitles, clip-art arrows, a drop shadow on everything. Timing is slightly off the beat on purpose. End with "Thank you for watching!!!" bouncing in. It should look like 2003 PowerPoint, but be readable and funny.
```

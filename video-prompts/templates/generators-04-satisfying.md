# Залипательное: ASMR, лупы, эстетика

**Для:** Veo · Sora · Kling · Runway · Seedance · Hailuo · Wan

Ролики, которые смотрят по кругу. Делай их бесшовными: последний кадр должен совпадать с первым.

Замени всё в `[КВАДРАТНЫХ СКОБКАХ]` на своё. Остальное копируй как есть.

## Резка кинетического песка

Нож режет цветной песок, слои рассыпаются. `9:16` · ~8 с

```text
Close-up ASMR: a sharp knife slowly slices through a block of layered [COLOURS] kinetic sand; the slice crumbles in satisfying slow detail. Top-lit studio, soft shadows, clean white surface. Sound: crisp crunchy slicing, no music.
```

## Бесшовный луп

Объект вращается/переливается и замыкает цикл. `1:1` · ~6 с

```text
Seamless loop: [OBJECT: an iridescent glass sphere / a woven knot / a stack of rings] rotates and morphs in a perfectly repeating cycle; the final frame is identical to the first. Pastel gradient background, soft studio light, ray-traced reflections. Calm, hypnotic.
```

## Уютное окно и дождь

Lo-fi-атмосфера: чай, лампа, капли. `16:9` · ~10 с

```text
Static shot from inside a cosy [ROOM] at night: rain streams down a large window, city lights blur outside, a steaming cup of tea and an open book on the windowsill, a warm lamp glows, a cat sleeps nearby. Very slow subtle movement only (steam, rain, tail twitch). Lo-fi aesthetic, seamless loop. Sound: rain, soft crackle.
```

## Миниатюрный мир

Тилт-шифт: город/кухня как игрушечные. `16:9` · ~8 с

```text
Tilt-shift miniature effect: a tiny [SCENE: bustling food market / train station / construction site] seen from above at an angle, little people and vehicles moving slightly fast. Strong blur top and bottom, saturated toy-like colours, bright sun. Sound: playful ambient bustle.
```

## Заливка красками

Акриловые краски растекаются узорами. `9:16` · ~8 с

```text
Top-down macro of an acrylic paint pour: [COLOURS] paints flow and swirl over a canvas, forming cells and marbled patterns; a gentle tilt makes them spread to the edges. Glossy wet surface, bright even light. Sound: soft liquid trickles.
```

## Готовка ASMR

Без лиц, только руки, звук и красивые ингредиенты. `9:16` · ~10 с

```text
Overhead and close-up shots, hands only: preparing [DISH] step by step — chopping [INGREDIENT], cracking an egg, butter melting in a pan, final plating. Natural window light, wooden board, minimal props. Sound: amplified ASMR — chopping, sizzling, pouring. No music, no faces.
```

## Цепная реакция

Домино, шарики, механизмы — эффект Голдберга. `16:9` · ~10 с

```text
A long tracking shot following a chain reaction: dominoes fall into a marble run, the marble hits a lever that tips a cup, which rings a tiny bell, ending with [FINAL EVENT, e.g. a flag rising with the text "[TEXT]"]. Bright playroom set, crisp focus, smooth camera follow. Sound: clicks, rolls and a final ding.
```

## Эстетичный рум-тур

Плавный проезд по комнате мечты. `9:16` · ~10 с

```text
Smooth gimbal walk-through of a [STYLE: Japandi / cottagecore / Y2K] [ROOM TYPE] at golden hour: linen curtains moving, plants, a record player spinning, sunlight patterns on the wall. Slow, dreamy, film-like colour. Sound: soft lo-fi ambience.
```

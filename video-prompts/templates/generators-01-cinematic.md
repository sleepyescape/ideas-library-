# Кинематографичные кадры

**Для:** Veo · Sora · Kling · Runway · Seedance · Hailuo · Wan

Один кадр 5–10 секунд. Чем точнее камера, свет и действие, тем лучше. Склеивай несколько кадров в ролик.

Замени всё в `[КВАДРАТНЫХ СКОБКАХ]` на своё. Остальное копируй как есть.

## Герой в кадре: наезд

Медленный наезд на героя, который оборачивается. `16:9` · ~8 с

```text
Slow dolly-in, eye level, 35mm lens. [CHARACTER: age, clothing, one distinctive detail] stands with their back to camera in [LOCATION], then slowly turns to face the lens. Golden hour backlight, rim light on hair, shallow depth of field, gentle haze. Cinematic colour grade, teal shadows, warm highlights. Sound: distant wind, a low swelling note.
```

## Дрон над локацией

Открывающий общий план сверху. `16:9` · ~8 с

```text
Aerial drone shot, high altitude, slowly gliding forward and tilting down over [LANDSCAPE / CITY] at [TIME OF DAY]. [WEATHER: low fog / light rain / clear sky]. A single [OBJECT: car / boat / person] moves through the scene to give scale. Photorealistic, natural colours, 4K documentary look. Sound: ambient wind and faint [AMBIENT SOUND].
```

## Облёт продукта

Камера кружит вокруг предмета на подиуме. `1:1` · ~6 с

```text
Smooth 180° orbit around [PRODUCT] standing on a [MATERIAL] pedestal in a minimal studio. Soft key light from the left, a moving light sweep reveals reflections on the surface. Dark gradient background, subtle floating dust particles. Product stays centred and sharp. Commercial, premium, 8K detail. Sound: soft whoosh and a low synth hit at the end.
```

## Неоновая улица под дождём

Киберпанк-атмосфера, отражения в лужах. `9:16` · ~8 с

```text
Low-angle tracking shot following [CHARACTER] walking down a narrow neon-lit street at night in heavy rain. Reflections of pink and cyan signs in puddles, steam rising from vents, umbrellas passing by. Handheld feel, slight motion blur, anamorphic lens flares. Blade-Runner-like mood but original signage with no real brands. Sound: rain, distant traffic, muffled music.
```

## Макро-деталь

Крупный план текстуры: капли, ткани, механизм. `16:9` · ~6 с

```text
Extreme macro shot, slow slide left. [SUBJECT: dew drops on a leaf / coffee crema / watch gears / fabric weave] fills the frame; tiny details are razor-sharp while the background melts into creamy bokeh. Soft natural light, high micro-contrast. Slow-motion feel. Sound: a quiet, close [SOUND: drip / tick / rustle].
```

## Таймлапс изменения

День сменяется ночью, город загорается огнями. `16:9` · ~8 с

```text
Static wide shot, time-lapse of [PLACE] from [START: sunset] to [END: night]. Clouds streak across the sky, shadows sweep, lights switch on window by window, traffic becomes light trails. Photorealistic, locked-off camera, smooth exposure transition. Sound: an ambient track with rising tension.
```

## Пролёт сквозь пространство

Камера влетает в окно и пролетает сквозь комнаты. `16:9` · ~10 с

```text
Continuous FPV-style camera move: start outside [BUILDING], fly through an open window, glide through [ROOM 1] and [ROOM 2] past people [ACTIVITY], and end on a close-up of [FINAL OBJECT]. No cuts, steady and smooth, slight speed ramps at doorways. Warm interior light. Sound: muffled room tones changing as the camera passes.
```

## Силуэт на закате

Эпичный силуэт на фоне огромного неба. `21:9` · ~8 с

```text
Wide static shot, [CHARACTER] in silhouette standing on [CLIFF / ROOFTOP / DUNE] against a huge [SUNSET / STORM / AURORA] sky. Their coat moves in the wind; birds cross the frame. Epic scale, the character is small in the lower third. Rich gradient sky, film grain. Sound: wind and a deep orchestral swell.
```

## Замедленный удар

Брызги, осколки, порошок в slow motion. `16:9` · ~6 с

```text
Ultra slow-motion, 1000 fps look. [OBJECT: a strawberry / paint balloon / glass of water] [ACTION: drops into milk / bursts / shatters] against a black background; droplets and fragments freeze mid-air in crisp detail. Hard side light, high contrast. Camera slowly pushes in. Sound: deep, stretched impact with a long tail.
```

## Головокружение (dolly zoom)

Фон «растягивается» вокруг героя — момент осознания. `16:9` · ~5 с

```text
Dolly zoom (vertigo effect): the camera pulls back while zooming in, so [CHARACTER] stays the same size and the background of [LOCATION] stretches away. Their expression shifts to sudden realisation. Moody lighting, cinematic grade. Sound: a rising dissonant tone that cuts to silence.
```

# Тренды и вирусные форматы

**Для:** Veo · Sora · Kling · Runway · Seedance · Hailuo · Wan

Форматы, которые часто заходят в ленте. Делай свою версию, а не копию чужого ролика.

Замени всё в `[КВАДРАТНЫХ СКОБКАХ]` на своё. Остальное копируй как есть.

## Эволюция через века

Предмет/место морфит через эпохи. `9:16` · ~10 с

```text
Continuous morph, static camera: [OBJECT / PLACE] evolves through time — [ERA 1] → [ERA 2] → [ERA 3] → [ERA 4] → futuristic version. Each stage ~2 s, transformations flow seamlessly (materials, colours and shapes change). A year counter in the corner. Photorealistic, consistent framing.
```

## А что, если…

Фантазийный сценарий: «Что, если бы Луна была ближе». `9:16` · ~10 с

```text
"What if [SCENARIO]?" — a photorealistic shot of [EVERYDAY PLACE] where [CONSEQUENCE] has happened: people stop and stare, the camera slowly tilts up to reveal [BIG VISUAL]. Natural daylight, documentary realism, awe-inspiring scale. Sound: city ambience fading into a deep hum.
```

## Вид от первого лица

Камера — глаза героя: руки в кадре, действие. `9:16` · ~8 с

```text
First-person POV, GoPro-like wide lens: your hands [ACTION: grab a ladder / open a mysterious door / pet a dragon] in [PLACE]. Head-bob motion, natural breathing rhythm, immersive. [LIGHTING]. Sound: footsteps, breathing, [AMBIENT].
```

## Уличное интервью

Ведущий с микрофоном задаёт вопрос прохожему. `9:16` · ~8 с

```text
Street interview style, vertical. A host holds a microphone toward a fictional passer-by ([DESCRIPTION]) on a busy [CITY] street and asks: "[QUESTION]". The passer-by answers with a funny, unexpected reply: "[ANSWER]". Handheld, natural light, background crowd, burned-in captions.
```

## Преображение (глоу-ап)

Комната/вещь/персонаж: «было → стало» одним махом. `9:16` · ~8 с

```text
Before/after transformation in one move: start on a [BEFORE: messy room / rusty bike / plain sketch]; a hand swipes across the lens and the scene becomes [AFTER: the redesigned version]. Same camera position and framing in both halves. Satisfying, bright, crisp.
```

## Пародия на документалку

Голос «как у натуралиста» про офис/кота/себя. `16:9` · ~10 с

```text
Wildlife documentary style, telephoto lens with slight shake, as if filmed from hiding. Subject: [EVERYDAY SUBJECT: an office worker at the coffee machine / a toddler / a cat]. Calm British-style narrator (original voice, not a real person): "[NARRATION LINE]". The subject does [ACTION] at the funniest moment. Natural light, documentary grade.
```

## Гиперлапс путешествия

Быстрый проезд через город/страну за секунды. `9:16` · ~10 с

```text
Hyperlapse, moving forward fast: a journey from [START] to [END] — streets, landmarks (generic, not real brands), changing weather and day to night, compressed into 10 seconds with smooth stabilised motion and occasional speed ramps. Vibrant, energetic. Sound: rising rhythmic beat.
```

## Маскот реагирует на тренд

Твой фирменный герой повторяет популярный жест/танец. `9:16` · ~8 с

```text
[CHARACTER LINE] performs [TREND ACTION, described in your own words: a simple dance / reaction / gesture] in front of [BACKGROUND], hitting the moves on the beat, ending with a wink to camera. Bright, clean lighting, full-body shot, character design kept exactly consistent. Sound: upbeat original track.
```

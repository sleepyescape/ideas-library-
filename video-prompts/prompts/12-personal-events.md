# Личное: резюме, поздравления, события

Видео о себе, приглашения, поздравления, итоги года, путешествия.

## Видео-резюме для рекрутера
- **Где:** Claude Motion
- **Формат:** 20 с · 16:9 и 9:16
- **Теги:** resume, recruiter, personal

```text
A 20-second motion self-introduction for recruiters.
Name: [NAME]. Role: [ROLE]. 3 skills: [SKILL 1], [SKILL 2], [SKILL 3]. 2 achievements with numbers: [ACHIEVEMENT 1], [ACHIEVEMENT 2].
End with a contact card: [EMAIL] · [LINKEDIN].
Confident, clean, modern; my photo [ATTACH] in a circle at the start.
```

## Портфолио-шоурил
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 30 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** portfolio, showreel, designer

```text
A 30-second showreel of my work: [ATTACH 6–10 IMAGES / PROJECT LINKS].
Each project gets 2–3 seconds with a smart transition that matches shapes or colours between projects.
Opening card: "[NAME] — [DISCIPLINE]". Closing: "[WEBSITE]".
Cut to an upbeat track at 110 BPM, project names small in the corner.
```

## Приглашение на свадьбу/праздник
- **Где:** Claude Motion
- **Формат:** 20 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** invitation, wedding, party

```text
A 20-second animated invitation to [EVENT: our wedding / birthday / housewarming].
[NAMES] invite you on [DATE] at [TIME], [VENUE, address].
Elegant style: [STYLE, e.g. watercolour florals / gold foil / minimalist]. Botanical illustrations grow along the edges, text appears with handwriting animation.
End: "RSVP by [DATE]: [CONTACT]" and a small map pin.
```

## Поздравление с днём рождения
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Veo, Kling
- **Формат:** 15 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** birthday, greeting, fun

```text
A 15-second birthday greeting for [NAME], who loves [HOBBY / THING].
Their favourite thing becomes the decoration: [e.g. cats carry the cake, a guitar plays "Happy birthday"].

Message: "[PERSONAL MESSAGE]". Bright, cheerful, silly.
```

## Итоги года (личные)
- **Где:** Claude Motion
- **Формат:** 45 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** year-in-review, personal, recap

```text
My [YEAR] in 45 seconds. Month by month, one highlight each: [JAN – …], [FEB – …], … [DEC – …].
Each month is a photo [ATTACH] in a polaroid frame with a handwritten caption, flipping like a calendar.
Finish with "Best part: [THING]" and "Hello, [NEXT YEAR]".
Warm film-photo look, pastel tape, soft acoustic music.
```

## Трибьют / «спасибо» команде или учителю
- **Где:** Claude Motion
- **Формат:** 40 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** tribute, thank-you, emotional

```text
A 40-second thank-you video for [PERSON / TEAM] from [WHO].
Collage of photos [ATTACH] that drift slowly with Ken Burns motion; short quotes from people appear between them ([QUOTE 1], [QUOTE 2], [QUOTE 3]).
End: "Thank you, [NAME]" in large warm type.

```

## Обратный отсчёт до события

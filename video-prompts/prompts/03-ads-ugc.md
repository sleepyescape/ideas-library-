# Реклама, промо и UGC

Короткая реклама товаров и услуг: проблема → решение → призыв. Часть шаблонов — для
видеогенераторов (Veo, Sora, Kling, Seedance, Runway), часть — для анимации (Claude Motion).

## Проблема → решение → CTA (15 с)
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 15 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** ad, direct-response, cta

```text
A 15-second vertical ad for [PRODUCT].
0–3s hook: "[PAINFUL QUESTION]?" in huge type, shaking slightly.
3–9s: the pain, shown as 2 quick animated scenes ([PAIN 1], [PAIN 2]).
9–13s: [PRODUCT] appears and fixes it; show the result as a number or a happy visual.
13–15s: "[OFFER]" + "[CTA]" button that pulses.
Captions always on, safe area respected, brand colour [HEX].
```

## UGC-отзыв «как будто снято на телефон»
- **Где:** Veo, Sora, Kling, Seedance
- **Формат:** 8–10 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** ugc, testimonial, ai-video

```text
Handheld vertical selfie video, shot on a phone. A [AGE]-year-old [PERSON DESCRIPTION] sits in [PLACE, e.g. their car / kitchen], natural window light, slightly messy background.
They hold up [PRODUCT] to the camera and say, enthusiastic but casual: "[LINE, max 20 words]".
Small natural camera shake, quick glance away and back, authentic smile.
Audio: their voice only, room tone, no music.
```

## Товар на столе: «распаковка»
- **Где:** Veo, Sora, Kling
- **Формат:** 8 с · 9:16 или 1:1
- **Теги:** unboxing, product, ai-video

```text
Top-down shot of a wooden table. Two hands open a [PACKAGING] and lift out [PRODUCT], turning it slowly to catch the light.
Soft daylight from the left, shallow depth of field, crisp detail on [KEY DETAIL].
Satisfying sounds: paper rustle, box lid, a soft click.
Camera: static, then a slow push-in on the product in the last 2 seconds.
```

## Продуктовый «герой-кадр»
- **Где:** Veo, Sora, Kling, Runway
- **Формат:** 6–8 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** hero-shot, commercial, cinematic

```text
Premium commercial hero shot of [PRODUCT] on [SURFACE: wet black stone / marble / sand].
[ELEMENT: water splash / falling petals / slow smoke] moves around it in slow motion (120 fps look).
Camera: slow 180° orbit at product height, macro lens, f/2.8.
Lighting: dark studio, rim light in [COLOUR], one soft key from above.
No text, no people. Ends on a clean frame with space on the right for a logo.
```

## Еда: аппетитный крупный план
- **Где:** Veo, Sora, Kling
- **Формат:** 6 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** food, macro, ai-video

```text
Macro close-up of [DISH], freshly made. [ACTION: cheese stretching / syrup pouring / steam rising / a knife slicing through].
Warm tungsten light, dark background, shallow depth of field, slow motion.
Camera slowly pushes in. Rich sizzling and pouring sounds.
Style: high-end food commercial.
```

## Акция / распродажа
- **Где:** Claude Motion
- **Формат:** 10 с · 9:16 и 1:1
- **Теги:** sale, promo, discount

```text
A 10-second promo for [STORE]'s [SALE NAME].
The discount "-[N]%" smashes into frame, cracks the screen, and product cards ([PRODUCT 1], [PRODUCT 2], [PRODUCT 3]) fly through the cracks with old prices crossed out.
A countdown "Ends [DATE]" ticks in the corner.
Final card: "[PROMO CODE]" + "[CTA]".
Loud, bold, red and yellow, beat-synced hits.
```

## Отзывы клиентов каруселью
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 20 с · 1:1
- **Теги:** testimonials, social-proof

```text
A 20-second social-proof video for [BUSINESS].
Five review cards (name, star rating, one sentence) slide in one after another, each staying 3 seconds: [REVIEW 1] … [REVIEW 5].
Stars fill one by one; the rating total counts up to "[AVERAGE] ★ from [N] reviews".
End with the logo and "[CTA]".
Clean white cards on a soft [BRAND COLOUR] background.
```

## Местный бизнес: «зайдите к нам»
- **Где:** Veo, Sora, Kling
- **Формат:** 8 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** local, cafe, small-business

```text
Cosy cinematic shot inside [BUSINESS TYPE: a small coffee shop / bakery / barbershop] at [TIME OF DAY].
[ACTION: a barista pours latte art / bread comes out of the oven / a stylist finishes a cut] in the foreground; a few customers chat softly in the blurred background.
Warm golden light, slight film grain, slow dolly-in.
Natural ambient sound, gentle acoustic guitar.
```

## Реклама в стиле гравюры
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 20 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** ad, engraving, vintage

```text
A 20-second brand ad for [BRAND] in the style of an old engraving: crosshatched black ink on aged paper.
The illustration of [PRODUCT / SYMBOL] draws itself line by line, then the lines begin to move (gears turn, steam rises, leaves sway).
Ornamental frame and Victorian-style type: "[HEADLINE]".
Final beat: a wax seal stamps the logo.
```

## Сериал из трёх коротких реклам
- **Где:** Claude Motion
- **Формат:** 3 × 6 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** ad, series, bumper

```text
Make three 6-second bumper ads for [PRODUCT] that work as a series.
Each one: a single relatable situation ([SITUATION 1], [SITUATION 2], [SITUATION 3]) in 4 seconds, then the same end card "[TAGLINE]" + logo.
Same layout, font and end card across all three, a different accent colour per ad.
No voice-over; captions carry the joke.
```

## Анти-реклама (самоирония)
- **Где:** Claude Motion
- **Формат:** 20 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** ad, humor, honest

```text
A 20-second "honest ad" for [PRODUCT] that pokes fun at itself.
Classic ad clichés appear (a lens flare, "revolutionary!", a stock-photo smile) and are crossed out one by one by a hand-drawn red pen.
Then plain honest lines: "[HONEST LINE 1]", "[HONEST LINE 2]", "It just [WHAT IT DOES]."
End: logo + "[CTA]". Deadpan timing, white background, black type, red marker.
```

## Продукт в руке — реалистичный демо-кадр
- **Где:** Veo, Sora, Kling, Seedance
- **Формат:** 8 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** product-demo, hands, ai-video

```text
Close-up POV shot: a hand holds [PRODUCT] and demonstrates [ONE ACTION] in [SETTING].
Show the result clearly: [RESULT].
Bright natural daylight, realistic skin and reflections, handheld micro-movements.
Keep the product shape and logo consistent with the reference image [ATTACH IMAGE].
Ambient sound of the room plus the product's own sound.
```

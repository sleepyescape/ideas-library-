# Короткие ролики: Reels, TikTok, Shorts

Вертикальные 9:16, 7–60 секунд. Главное — крючок в первые 2 секунды и субтитры.
Шаблоны форматов, которые хорошо смотрятся сериями.

## Топ-5
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 30 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** list, top, countdown

```text
A 30-second vertical countdown: "Top 5 [TOPIC]".
Hook (0–2s): "#1 will surprise you" over a blurred preview of #1.
Each item gets 5 seconds: a big rank number flips in, the name, one visual, one fact.
Progress dots at the top show where we are.
End: "Which one did you know? 👇".
Energetic, bold numbers, colour changes per item, whoosh on each transition.
```

## «POV:» ситуация
- **Где:** Veo, Sora, Kling, Seedance
- **Формат:** 8 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** pov, relatable, ai-video

```text
First-person POV vertical video: you are [ROLE] at [PLACE] when [SITUATION].
Camera at eye level, natural head movement, hands visible at the bottom of the frame.
[WHAT HAPPENS in 2–3 beats].
Realistic lighting, ambient sound.
On-screen caption at the top: "POV: [CAPTION]".
```

## Факт, который взрывает мозг
- **Где:** Claude Motion
- **Формат:** 15 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** fact, curiosity, loop

```text
A 15-second vertical clip with one mind-blowing fact about [TOPIC]: "[FACT]".
Reveal the fact in three parts so each line raises curiosity.
Illustrate it with one simple, looping animation.
Make the last frame match the first so the video loops seamlessly.
Dark background, glowing type, one sound hit per line.
```

## Мини-туториал «3 шага»
- **Где:** Claude Motion
- **Формат:** 25 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** tutorial, how-to, steps

```text
A 25-second vertical tutorial: "How to [RESULT] in 3 steps".
Show the final result for 2 seconds first, then rewind effect.
Steps 1–3: big step number, a short instruction, an animated demo of the action.
End: the result again + "Save for later".
Clean, bright, big captions in the middle third, tick sound on each step.
```

## Сторителлинг «я сделал X за Y дней»
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 40 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** challenge, story, progress

```text
A 40-second vertical story: "I tried [CHALLENGE] for [N] days".
Day counter in the corner advances fast; progress bar fills.
Highlights: Day 1 ([FEELING]), Day [X] ([PROBLEM]), Day [Y] ([BREAKTHROUGH]), Day [N] ([RESULT]).
Each highlight is a 6-second card with a doodle illustration.
End with the before/after and "Would you try it?".
Notebook / diary aesthetic, handwritten font, marker highlights.
```

## Вопрос → ответ (для комментариев)
- **Где:** Claude Motion
- **Формат:** 20 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** reply, comment, engagement

```text
A 20-second vertical "reply to comment" video.
Show a comment bubble from @[USERNAME]: "[QUESTION]".
The bubble flies to the top, and the answer builds below in 3 animated points with tiny icons.
End: "Ask the next question in the comments".
Social-app look: rounded bubbles, system font, soft shadows.
```

## Ожидание vs реальность
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Veo, Kling
- **Формат:** 12 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** humor, expectation-reality, split

```text
A 12-second vertical split-screen: top half "Expectation", bottom half "Reality", about [TOPIC].
Top: [IDEAL VERSION], polished, glossy, slow motion.
Bottom: [REAL VERSION], chaotic, funny, plays at the same time.
At 9 seconds the top half glitches and collapses into the bottom one.
Labels in bold white with black outline.
```

## Залипательный луп
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 8 с · 9:16 (бесшовный)
- **Теги:** satisfying, loop, abstract

```text
An 8-second perfectly seamless loop that is oddly satisfying: [OBJECTS, e.g. coloured balls] [ACTION, e.g. drop into slots, sort themselves by colour, and reset].
Physics should feel weighty and exact. The last frame must equal the first.
Pastel colours, soft shadows, top-down view, satisfying click sounds.
```

## Таймлапс «из ничего в готовое»
- **Где:** Veo, Sora, Kling
- **Формат:** 8 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** timelapse, transformation, ai-video

```text
Timelapse vertical video: [EMPTY THING, e.g. an empty room / bare plot / blank canvas] transforms into [FINISHED THING] over 8 seconds.
Static camera on a tripod, light changes from morning to evening across the clip.
Workers / brush strokes / growth appear as fast motion blur.
Final second holds on the finished result.
```

## Тренд-шаблон «вещи, которые…»

## Хук-заставка для начала ролика
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 3 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** hook, intro, template

```text
Design a 3-second hook intro for my vertical videos about [NICHE].
The hook line "[HOOK]" slams in word by word with screen shake; a short whoosh-hit sound.
Leave the bottom third empty so it can be layered over talking-head footage.
Transparent background (export with alpha), white type with black stroke, [ACCENT] highlight on the key word.
```

## Субтитры «как у блогеров»
- **Где:** Remotion, Claude Code
- **Формат:** любой · 9:16
- **Теги:** captions, talking-head, edit

```text
Add word-by-word animated captions to my talking-head video [FILE].
Transcribe with timestamps, show 1–3 words at a time in the centre-lower third.
The current word pops (scale 1.15) and turns [ACCENT COLOUR]; key words get an emoji.
Font: heavy rounded sans, white with black stroke and soft shadow.
Also cut silences longer than 0.4 s and add a subtle zoom-in on punchlines.
```

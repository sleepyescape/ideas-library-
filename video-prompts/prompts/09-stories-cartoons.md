# Истории и мультики

Персонажи, короткие сюжеты, сказки, скетчи. Для стабильного героя держите одно описание
внешности и вставляйте его дословно в каждый промпт (см. плагин `cartoon-reel`).

## Мини-мультик с двумя героями
- **Где:** Veo, Kling, Seedance, Sora
- **Формат:** 6–10 планов по 5 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** cartoon, dialogue, series

```text
Shot [N] of [TOTAL]. Style: [STYLE, e.g. 3D Pixar-like / flat 2D / claymation], consistent across all shots.
Characters: [HERO: one-line appearance], [SIDEKICK: one-line appearance].
Setting: [PLACE].
Action: [WHAT HAPPENS in this shot].
Dialogue: [CHARACTER]: "[LINE, max 12 words]".
Camera: [wide / medium / close-up], [static / slow push-in].
Mood: [MOOD]. Keep faces, colours and proportions identical to previous shots.
```

## Немое кино: смотритель маяка
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Veo, Kling
- **Формат:** 60 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** silent, short-film, emotional

```text
A 60-second silent short film with no dialogue: [CHARACTER] who [SITUATION, e.g. keeps a lighthouse alone] and [SMALL EVENT that changes their day].
Tell it in 6 scenes through action and expression only: routine → disruption → attempt → failure → small kindness → new routine.
Muted palette with one warm colour that grows across the film. Piano score, ambient sounds.
```

```text
Tell [STORY / FABLE] as a shadow-puppet play.
```

## Сериал «маленький робот»

- **Формат:** 8 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** robot, series, wholesome

```text
Episode beat: a small rusty robot with one antenna and round blue eyes [ACTION, e.g. tries to water a single flower growing through cracked concrete] in [SETTING, e.g. an abandoned city at sunset].
```

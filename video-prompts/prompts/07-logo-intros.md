# Логотипы, интро и заставки

Короткие 3–10-секундные ролики: появление логотипа, интро канала, аутро, переходы.

## Медленная сборка логотипа
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 8 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** logo, reveal, slow

```text
An impressive 8-second slow-reveal motion design that assembles and finally reveals the [BRAND] logo [ATTACH SVG].
Start with abstract fragments of the logo's shapes drifting in darkness; they find each other, align and lock with satisfying precision.
Last 2 seconds: light sweep across the finished logo, then the tagline "[TAGLINE]" fades in.
```

## Логотип из частиц
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion, Three.js
- **Формат:** 6 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** logo, particles, tech

```text
A 6-second logo reveal: thousands of tiny particles swirl like a galaxy, then are pulled into the shape of the [BRAND] logo [ATTACH SVG].
Once formed, the logo glows briefly and the particles settle into solid fill.
Dark background, particles in [BRAND COLOUR], deep whoosh into a soft hit.
```

- **Формат:** 5 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** intro, youtube, channel

```text
A 5-second YouTube intro for the channel "[CHANNEL NAME]" about [TOPIC].
Quick montage of 4 animated icons representing the topic ([ICON 1], [ICON 2], [ICON 3], [ICON 4]), each on a beat, then the channel name slams in with a sticker-like bounce.

```

## Аутро с подпиской
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 15 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** outro, end-screen, youtube

```text
A 15-second YouTube end screen for [CHANNEL].
Left: two empty rounded rectangles for recommended videos (placeholder frames). Right: a circle for the subscribe button with the avatar.
Background: slow looping animation in channel colours [HEX].
Text: "Thanks for watching" fades in at the top. Everything settles by second 2 and stays still.
```

## Логотип из жидкости

```text
Photorealistic macro shot: glossy [LIQUID: ink / paint / molten gold] flows across a dark surface, swirls, and settles into the exact shape of the logo in the reference image [ATTACH LOGO].
```

## Переход-шторка для видео
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 1 с · 16:9 и 9:16
- **Теги:** transition, wipe, pack

```text
Make a pack of 6 one-second transitions in my brand colours ([HEX 1], [HEX 2], [HEX 3]): diagonal wipe, circle iris, stacked bars, liquid blob, pixel dissolve, shape zoom.
Each must fully cover the frame at the midpoint (frame 12 of 24) so I can cut underneath.
Export with alpha.
```

# Кинематографичные кадры (видеогенераторы)

Шаблоны для Veo, Sora, Kling, Seedance, Runway, Luma, Hailuo. Формула:
**объект + действие + место + камера + свет + стиль + звук.** Один кадр — одна мысль, 5–10 секунд.

## Универсальная формула кадра
- **Где:** Veo, Sora, Kling, Seedance, Runway
- **Формат:** 5–10 с · любой
- **Теги:** formula, base, cinematic

```text
[SHOT TYPE: wide / medium / close-up / extreme close-up] of [SUBJECT with 2–3 visual details] [ACTION] in [LOCATION], [TIME OF DAY / WEATHER].
Camera: [MOVE: static / slow dolly-in / tracking / orbit / crane up / handheld], [LENS: 24mm / 50mm / 85mm], [DEPTH OF FIELD].
Lighting: [KEY LIGHT], [COLOUR PALETTE].
Style: [FILM LOOK, e.g. 35mm film, Kodak Portra, anamorphic].
Audio: [AMBIENT SOUNDS], [MUSIC or none], [DIALOGUE in quotes if any].
```

## Эпичный пейзаж с дрона
- **Где:** Veo, Sora, Kling, Runway
- **Формат:** 8 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** drone, landscape, epic

```text
Aerial drone shot flying low over [LANDSCAPE: fjord / desert dunes / rice terraces / volcanic coast] at [golden hour / blue hour], then rising slowly to reveal [REVEAL: a lone tower / the ocean / a city].
Volumetric light, mist in the valleys, ultra-detailed, smooth gimbal motion.
Sound: wind and distant birds, swelling orchestral note at the reveal.
```

## Трейлер: героический кадр
- **Где:** Veo, Sora, Kling
- **Формат:** 6 с · 2.39:1
- **Теги:** trailer, hero, epic

```text
Low-angle slow-motion shot of [CHARACTER: appearance, costume] walking towards camera through [ENVIRONMENT: smoke / rain / falling ash], [ACTION: drawing a sword / taking off a helmet].
Backlit by [LIGHT SOURCE], rim light on the silhouette, anamorphic lens flare.
Letterboxed 2.39:1, teal-and-orange grade, deep trailer boom on the last step.
```

## Нуар в дождь
- **Где:** Veo, Sora, Kling, Runway
- **Формат:** 8 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** noir, rain, moody

```text
Black-and-white film-noir shot: a detective in a trench coat lights a cigarette under a streetlamp on a rain-soaked street at night, [YEAR]-era city.
Hard side light through venetian blinds shadow pattern on the wall, reflections in puddles, steam from a grate.
Camera: slow push-in from medium to close-up. Sound: rain, distant jazz saxophone, the lighter click.
```

## Киберпанк-город
- **Где:** Veo, Sora, Kling, Seedance
- **Формат:** 8 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** cyberpunk, neon, city

```text
Tracking shot following [CHARACTER] through a dense cyberpunk market at night: holographic signs in [LANGUAGE], steam, crowds with umbrellas, flying vehicles overhead.
Neon magenta and cyan reflections on wet asphalt, shallow depth of field, slight handheld.
Sound: rain on umbrellas, chatter, synth bass hum.
```

## Природа: макро
- **Где:** Veo, Sora, Kling
- **Формат:** 6 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** nature, macro, documentary

```text
Extreme macro nature documentary shot: [SUBJECT: a dew drop on a spider web / a bee landing on lavender / a snail on moss] at sunrise.
Razor-thin depth of field, soft bokeh, natural light, ultra-sharp detail.
Camera barely moves (slow focus pull from background to subject).
Sound: quiet morning forest ambience.
```

## Ностальгия на плёнку
- **Где:** Veo, Sora, Kling
- **Формат:** 8 с · 4:3
- **Теги:** vintage, 8mm, nostalgia

```text
Super 8 home-movie footage from [DECADE]: [SCENE: kids running through a sprinkler / a family picnic / a road trip in an old car].
Warm faded colours, heavy grain, light leaks, slight gate weave and flicker, 4:3 frame with rounded corners.
Handheld, a bit overexposed. Sound: projector whir, muffled laughter.
```

## Сюрреализм
- **Где:** Veo, Sora, Kling, Runway
- **Формат:** 8 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** surreal, dreamlike, art

```text
Surreal dreamlike shot: [IMPOSSIBLE SCENE, e.g. a whale swims slowly through clouds above a quiet village / a staircase descends into the ocean / a room fills with floating oranges].
Calm, matter-of-fact atmosphere, pastel palette, soft diffuse light, like a Magritte painting come to life.
Slow steady camera, no cuts. Sound: soft ambient pad and distant bells.
```

## Пролёт сквозь объекты (FPV)
- **Где:** Veo, Kling, Seedance
- **Формат:** 8 с · 16:9 или 9:16
- **Теги:** fpv, flythrough, dynamic

```text
One continuous FPV drone shot: start outside [BUILDING / PLACE], fly through an open window, weave through [ROOMS / CROWD / KITCHEN], dive through [GAP] and burst out into [FINAL VIEW].
Fast, smooth, close to surfaces, motion blur, no cuts.
Bright realistic lighting. Sound: drone buzz and environment noises changing with each space.
```

## Модный фэшн-кадр
- **Где:** Veo, Sora, Kling
- **Формат:** 6 с · 9:16
- **Теги:** fashion, editorial, model

```text
High-fashion editorial video: a model in [OUTFIT with details] walks towards camera on [LOCATION: a salt flat / brutalist rooftop / empty runway], wind moving the fabric.
Camera: slow-motion, 85mm, tracking backwards at eye level.
Lighting: [hard midday sun with deep shadows / soft overcast]. Colour grade: [STYLE].
Sound: minimal techno beat.
```

## Изображение → видео (оживить фото)
- **Где:** Kling, Runway, Veo, Luma
- **Формат:** 5 с · как у фото
- **Теги:** image-to-video, animate-photo

```text
[ATTACH IMAGE]. Animate this image subtly and realistically.
Motion: [WHAT MOVES: hair and clothes in a light breeze / clouds drift / water ripples / the person turns their head and smiles].
Camera: [slow push-in / gentle parallax / static].
Keep the face, composition and colours exactly as in the image; no new objects.
```

## Первый и последний кадр (переход)
- **Где:** Kling, Veo, Luma, Runway
- **Формат:** 5 с · любой
- **Теги:** keyframes, transition, morph

```text
Start frame: [ATTACH IMAGE A]. End frame: [ATTACH IMAGE B].
Create a smooth, physically plausible transition between them: [HOW: the camera flies forward through / the object transforms / day turns into night].
Keep everything that exists in both frames stable; no flicker.
```

## Многокадровая сцена с диалогом
- **Где:** Veo, Sora, Seedance
- **Формат:** 10–15 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** multishot, dialogue, scene

```text
A short scene in [N] shots, consistent characters and lighting.
Characters: [A: appearance, voice], [B: appearance, voice]. Location: [PLACE], [TIME].
Shot 1 (wide, 3s): [ESTABLISHING ACTION].
Shot 2 (over-the-shoulder on A, 4s): A says "[LINE]".
Shot 3 (close-up on B, 4s): B reacts and says "[LINE]".
Shot 4 (wide, 3s): [ENDING ACTION].
Style: [FILM LOOK]. Ambient sound: [SOUNDS]. No music.
```

# Кинетическая типографика

Ролики, где главный герой — текст: цитаты, манифесты, лирик-видео, титры.
Идеально для Claude Motion и кода.

## Цитата крупным шрифтом
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 15 с · 9:16 или 1:1
- **Теги:** quote, typography, minimal

```text
A 15-second kinetic typography piece for the quote: "[QUOTE]" — [AUTHOR].
Words arrive in rhythm with natural speech; the 2–3 key words are larger and in [ACCENT COLOUR].
Use scale, weight and position changes rather than fades. Each line clears with a sharp mask wipe.
Hold the full quote for the last 3 seconds, then the author name.
Black background, white grotesque type, no other elements.
```

## Манифест бренда
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 45 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** manifesto, brand, typography

```text
A 45-second manifesto film for [BRAND] made only of type.
Text: [PASTE MANIFESTO, 6–10 short lines].
Each line gets its own treatment: one stretches, one stacks, one is cut by a moving bar, one fills the whole frame letter by letter.
Build tension towards the final line, then silence and the logo.
Two colours only: [COLOUR 1] and [COLOUR 2], swapping background/foreground on key lines.
```

## Лирик-видео под трек
- **Где:** Claude Code + Remotion
- **Формат:** длина трека · 16:9 или 9:16
- **Теги:** lyrics, music, sync

```text
Make a lyric video for [SONG FILE] with these lyrics: [PASTE LYRICS].
Detect beats and align every line to its timestamp; words appear exactly when sung.
Verses: calm, small type, lower third. Chorus: huge full-screen type that pulses on the kick.
Change the colour palette at each new section. Background: slow abstract gradient reacting to volume.
```

## Хромовая типографика на бит
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 15 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** chrome, showreel, loud

```text
A 15-second showreel of loud kinetic typography in chrome and black.
Words: [WORD 1], [WORD 2], [WORD 3], [WORD 4], [BRAND].
Every word lands on a beat with a hard cut, metallic reflections sliding across the letters.
Mix extreme scales: one letter fills the screen, the next word is tiny in the corner.
Final: the brand name in chrome with a light sweep.
```

## Швейцарский стиль
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 15 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** swiss, grid, editorial

```text
A 15-second Swiss-style motion piece about [SUBJECT].
Strict 12-column grid visible as faint lines; red (#E30613) and black on white.
Helvetica-like type moves only along grid lines; blocks slide and snap.
Use numbers and arrows as graphic elements. Ends with a perfectly balanced poster frame.
```

## Слово меняет смысл
- **Где:** Claude Motion
- **Формат:** 10 с · 1:1
- **Теги:** wordplay, morph, typography

```text
A 10-second typographic animation where the word "[WORD 1]" morphs letter by letter into "[WORD 2]", and then into "[WORD 3]", telling a tiny story ([e.g. "dream" → "draft" → "done"]).
Letters that stay move smoothly to new positions; letters that change flip like a split-flap display.
Centred, large serif, cream background, dark ink.
```

## Титры фильма
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 60 с · 16:9 (2.39:1 кашетированный)
- **Теги:** titles, credits, cinematic

```text
An opening title sequence for a film called "[TITLE]", genre: [GENRE].
Credits ([NAMES AND ROLES]) appear one by one, integrated with abstract imagery that hints at the story ([MOTIF]).
Pacing: slow and moody; each credit holds ~3 seconds.
Typography in [STYLE, e.g. condensed serif], letterboxed 2.39:1, film grain, the title last and largest.
```

## Текст набирается в терминале
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 20 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** terminal, hacker, typing

```text
A 20-second terminal animation. A prompt "$" blinks, then types: [COMMANDS / MESSAGE].
Output lines stream fast after each command, with realistic delays.
At the end the screen clears and ASCII art of "[NAME / LOGO]" draws itself line by line.
Green-on-black CRT look with scanlines and slight glow.
```

## Поэма, проявляющаяся строками
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 40 с · 16:9 или 9:16
- **Теги:** poem, slow, editorial

```text
Animate this short poem: [PASTE POEM].
Each line becomes legible slowly, as if ink soaks into paper; previous lines dim but stay.
Gentle camera drift; one small illustrated motif ([MOTIF]) grows in the margin as the poem progresses.
Paper texture, serif type, muted palette, calm ambient pad.
```

## Глитч-заголовок
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 5 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** glitch, title, cyber

```text
A 5-second glitch title for "[TITLE]".
The headline splits into RGB channels, slices displace horizontally, scanline noise flickers, then it snaps back clean.
Repeat the glitch twice briefly, the second time revealing a subtitle "[SUBTITLE]".
Black background, cobalt and vermilion channel offsets, digital crackle sound.
```

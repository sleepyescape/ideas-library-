# Запуски продуктов и приложений

Ролики для лендинга, X/Twitter, Product Hunt и магазинов приложений: интерфейс, фичи, логотип в финале.
Делаются в Claude Motion или кодом; скриншоты и брендбук прикладывайте к промпту.

## Запуск SaaS за 30 секунд
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 30 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** launch, saas, ui

```text
A 30-second launch video for [PRODUCT], a [ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION] for [AUDIENCE].
0–5s: the problem in one bold line: "[PROBLEM]".
5–22s: three features, about 5 seconds each: [FEATURE 1], [FEATURE 2], [FEATURE 3]. Rebuild each as a clean UI card (not a screenshot) that animates the action a user would take.
22–30s: the logo assembles on the beat, then the URL "[URL]" and "[CTA]".
Brand colours [HEX 1] and [HEX 2], font [FONT], cuts synced to a 120 BPM track.
```

## Одна фигура — весь интерфейс
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 20 с · 16:9
- **Теги:** launch, morph, ui, minimal

```text
A 20-second product film where one shape morphs continuously into every key UI element of [PRODUCT]: a button, then [ELEMENT 2], [ELEMENT 3], [ELEMENT 4], and finally the logo.
A cursor drives every change, clicking, dragging and scrolling.
Never cut: one continuous take.
Black and white only, with a single accent colour on the final logo. Spring easing with a slight overshoot.
```

## Тизер новой функции
- **Где:** Claude Motion
- **Формат:** 15 с · 9:16 и 16:9
- **Теги:** teaser, feature, date

```text
A 15-second teaser for [FEATURE NAME], coming to [PRODUCT] on [DATE].
Show only glimpses: blurred UI fragments, a cropped close-up of [KEY ELEMENT], a cursor hovering but never clicking.
Text beats: "Something new" → "[HINT]" → the date revealed with a mask wipe.
Dark background, glowing edges, low hum building to a hit at the date.
Export both 9:16 and 16:9.
```

## Продукт из одной точки

## Продукт за один непрерывный кадр
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 20 с · 16:9

```text
A launch film for [PRODUCT] in one continuous take: photos, UI and type flow into each other with no cuts.
```

## Product Hunt: «Мы запустились!»
- **Где:** Claude Motion
- **Формат:** 20 с · 1:1
- **Теги:** product-hunt, launch-day, social

```text
A 20-second square "We're live on Product Hunt" clip for [PRODUCT].
Open with confetti bursting from the logo, then the tagline "[TAGLINE]".
Show three tiny looping feature demos in a row.
Finish with an upvote arrow that bounces, the text "Support us today" and [LINK].
Bright, celebratory, [BRAND COLOUR] with orange accents, bouncy easing.
```

## Запуск в бумажном стиле
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion

- **Теги:** launch, paper, craft

```text
A 15-second launch film for [PRODUCT] in a light paper style.
```

## Ролик-милестоун
- **Где:** Claude Motion, Remotion
- **Формат:** 15 с · 16:9 или 1:1
- **Теги:** milestone, numbers, celebration

```text
Celebrate [MILESTONE, e.g. 1,000,000 users] for [PRODUCT] in 15 seconds.
A counter ticks up from 0 with accelerating speed while a growth chart draws itself behind it.
At the target number, the counter freezes, the digits explode into particles that reform as "Thank you".
Finish with the logo.
Gold-on-black, sparkles on the peak, a drum roll that ends on a hit.
```
